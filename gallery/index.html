Gauss-Opt Gallery

Scenes reconstructed and trained in the browser by Gauss-Opt (WebGPU structure-from-motion, then Gaussian Splatting) from a handful of photos. Shown: the trained splats, the cameras the photos were taken from, the path through them, and the 3D points the reconstruction found. Fly-by follows the camera path (r: resume it); drag to orbit, shift-drag or right-drag (two fingers on a phone) to pan, wheel to zoom; f or a flight key: drone (drag = stick, ↑/↓ forward / back, ←/→ yaw, e/q climb / descend, space re-center, wheel = tilt); h: the URL options.
