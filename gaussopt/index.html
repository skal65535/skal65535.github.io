Start from a video or a set of photos (JPEG), or open a COLMAP gs/ folder (sparse/ + images/) or a checkpoint. Files can also be dropped on the page.

How to shoot a photo set
  • Walk, don't pivot: take each photo from a new spot. Photos turning from one spot give no depth.
  • Small steps: from one photo to the next, turn at most 30° and move at most half the distance to the subject. Around an object, that is 12 photos per full turn.
  • Overlap: every part of the scene in 3 photos or more: the more photos see a part, the better it looks from in between.
  • Lenses and zoom can be mixed (a camera model each), and so can portrait and landscape; a lens with fewer than 5 photos is not calibrated on its own.
  • Something to match: avoid photos of only a plain wall, a window or the sky.
